The matched family uses matte charcoal structural frames, satin warm-grey panels, restrained metal hardware, dark textured grips, orange foam muzzles, and blank neutral swappable engraving plates. No neon or emissive details. Dart-9 remains the compact sidearm; Buzzline the raised-bridge SMG; Ranger the AR-style rifle; Needlepoint the scoped bolt-action sniper rifle. Keep the strong phone-readable silhouettes and original fictional identity. Do not copy real product marks.

These are concept sheets, not internally consistent engineering drawings. In particular, Needlepoint's bipod is folded in its side view and extended in its front view; production should define one rest pose without changing the approved overall look. Roblox mesh budgets, attachment points, animations, imports, and phone performance still need implementation and verification.
