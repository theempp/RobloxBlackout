## Revised shapes

- **Ranger:** Split upper and lower receiver, flat top rail, straight/very gently curved box magazine, separate pistol grip, inline adjustable stock, long slotted free-float fore-end, foam orange muzzle. Avoid the prior deep curved magazine and AK-like continuous body. Make the gun original and fictional rather than copying a real branded AR model.
- **Needlepoint:** Heavy bolt-action precision silhouette: visibly long thick barrel with orange foam tip, substantial optic in raised rings, cheek-height adjustable stock, rear butt hook, short detachable magazine, visible bolt handle, and folded or compact bipod under the fore-end. Keep an open stock frame and blank engraving plate. Make it feel like a sniper-class sporting foam blaster, not a generic carbine stretched longer.

Both prompts will ask for an original fictional foam-dart design and the same material palette, studio background, side/front composition, and no text/branding as the existing four sheets. The prior images can be used as visual review references without being submitted to the model, avoiding shape lock-in from the current Ranger and Needlepoint.
