# Four-gun visual concept plan — approved and generated

The owner approved four GPT Image 2.5 medium/1k sheets at a 2.00-credit cap. All four were generated with no retries; see `RESULTS.md`. This is a new design exploration, separate from the existing blockout, detail, and color meshes. The current art, scripts, points, and game systems stay untouched.

## Direction

Make a matched family of premium **foam-dart sporting equipment** with the credibility of machined field instruments. Change the construction and proportions, not just the paint: a visible dark structural chassis, broad removable satin-metal shrouds, deep mechanical seams, restrained exposed fasteners, and coarse rubber only where hands touch. Keep enough foam-blaster language to read as fictional play equipment. No copied silhouettes, logos, or marks from the owner reference images; use `refs/guns/NOTES.md` only as role and shape guidance. Do not upload those reference images or the existing painted renders.

Shared palette: predominantly matte charcoal and bead-blasted graphite, with warm grey anodized panels and small brushed-steel hardware. Distinguish the guns through silhouette and mechanism, not jewel colors. Every gun gets one unmistakable orange foam muzzle tip and one plain medium-grey, swappable engraving plate on the left receiver. No neon, emissive, glow tubes, bright light strips, excessive gloss, weathering, camouflage, or text/logos. Large value blocks and sparse details must read on a phone.

## Deliverable and exact run

One 16:9 concept sheet for each gun (four generation jobs). Each sheet has a large clean left-side profile, a smaller head-on front view of the **same design**, and clear blank space at the engraving plate. Neutral warm-grey studio background; even soft light; no people, hands, props, scene, captions, fake labels, or branding. Gun names will be provided outside the generated images. Save accepted outputs under this folder and show all four inline for review. No 3D conversion, mesh editing, Studio upload, commit, or extra variant is in this approval.

Common prompt prefix for all four jobs:

> Original industrial-design concept sheet for a fictional premium foam-dart sporting blaster in the Blackout Crew game. Two consistent views of exactly one new design: dominant clean left-side profile and smaller head-on front view. Matte charcoal machined structural chassis, warm-grey satin anodized removable shell panels, brushed-steel service hardware, coarse dark rubber only on grip/contact points, visible believable seams and a few fasteners. Visibly orange foam muzzle tip. A plain neutral medium-grey rectangular swappable engraving plate on the left receiver, entirely blank. Strong simple silhouette and broad contrast that read on a phone. Realistic product-design rendering, controlled soft studio reflections, neutral warm-grey background. A credible finished object, clearly fictional foam equipment. No neon, emissive surfaces, glow, colored light strips, polished plastic, toy shine, jewels, wear/grime, camouflage, text, logos, hands, people, ammunition, or copied commercial weapon design.

Gun-specific prompt suffixes:

- **Dart-9:** Compact pistol-scale sidearm. Short wedge-shaped upper carriage above a distinct inset frame, deep open trigger loop, strong grip heel, a short orange foam nose, visible slide break and in-grip magazine base. New proportions rather than a plain rectangular slide.
- **Buzzline:** Compact SMG with low receiver under a raised carry bridge, vented front sleeve, angled hand grip, visibly forward-curved detachable magazine, and two-rail collapsed stock with padded end. Short flared orange foam nose.
- **Ranger:** Slim rifle with long tapered barrel sleeve and orange foam end, high continuous spine, one large diagonal opening in the handguard, open triangular buttstock with cheek pad, and substantial curved detachable magazine.
- **Needlepoint:** Long light marksman blaster with extended brushed-steel tube barrel ending in orange foam, protective top bridge and compact optic rail, visible bolt housing, small magazine, and open hooked thumbhole stock with deep cheek saddle.
