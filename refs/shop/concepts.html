BLACKOUT CREW · SHOP CONCEPTS v2

Restyled to match the quality of your references. All three are drawn at iPhone 16 Pro landscape size (874×402 pt). Glow is used on the 2D shop UI only. Guns, karts and the world stay no-neon, as your Sept 30 rule says. Item art here is shaded vector stand-ins. In the real game it would be renders of your actual 3D models, which is where the reference's 3D look comes from. (v1 is kept as concepts-v1.html.)

BUTTONS

Glossy 3-stop gradient with a hard shine line at the middle, a bright 2 px top bevel, a dark bottom lip, a black outline, an outer glow in the theme colour, and a hard drop shadow. Layout: cart │ coin + price │ BUY.

3D

Lit item renders with a coloured rim light, a contact shadow, glass and metal highlights, and a scene behind them (vault door, bokeh, floor bounce). Roblox: ViewportFrame or a pre-rendered image of the real model.

2D

Chamfered panels with gradient edges, two-tone headline type, rarity tags, ribbons, % OFF stickers, icon chips, numbered section tags and hazard stripes.

GLOW

Coloured light around the active tab, buy buttons, panel edges and title accents. Roblox: 9-slice glow images plus UIGradient/UIStroke. Cheap on phones, no real lights.
