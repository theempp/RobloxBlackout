WHAT CHANGED

  • Cool gray (Directive) frame instead of orange. Colour comes only from the 4 squad slots, used as flat bands.
  • The reference's 2×2 becomes a 3-module row: Spin (with pity counter + ODDS), Pass, Coin packs (Shop Section Modules #06).

FIT / RISK

  • The strongest season identity, and the easiest to re-skin each season (swap the bands and title).
  • Gray UI can read as “dull” to a 9–17 audience. The squad colours have to carry it.
  • Coin pack sizes and R$ prices are placeholders. The Robux side isn't set in Config yet.

APPLIES TO ALL THREE

  • No countdown timers. Bundles say “rotates Monday” (§5i: no countdowns; FOMO timers are risky for this age group).
  • The Spin tile switches to earnable tokens and hides Robux prices for PolicyService-restricted players. It's hidden completely once every item is owned.
  • Fonts: A = Oswald / Roboto Condensed, B = Luckiest Guy / Special Elite, C = Michroma / Roboto Mono. All are built into Roblox.
